## 1. What this CR lands

**1,196 Guardrail candidate submissions** in `contributions/queue/<shard>/<slug>.yaml` (sharded per entity-storage-layout.md §4; largest shard 60 files — cap-compliant), generated by the programme's `generate_queue.py` from one evidence bank:

| Bank | Source | Records |
|---|---|---|
| nist-800-53-rev5.json | NIST SP 800-53 Rev.5 control catalog (OSCAL JSON, usnistgov/oscal-content; 324 controls + 872 enhancements = 1,196) | 1,196 |

Provenance is recorded in `ingestion/manifest.yaml` (bank sha256, source URL, retrieval date, mapping note). Every submission carries `source_ref` (control identifier, e.g. `NIST SP 800-53 Rev.5 AC-2`), a `published-document` evidence citation to https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final, and the record's published statement/guidance prose as its description.

**Honest mappings**: `enforcement` (905 advisory / 179 automated-warn / 112 automated-block) and `domain` are provisional programmatic classifications from control family and title — documented in the manifest and in every record's `evidence.rationale` as subject to canonical-admission review. The published catalog does not mandate an enforcement mechanism.
